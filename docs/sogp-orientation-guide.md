# SOGP orientation guide

## How the programme works and how to use your dashboard

Welcome to the School of God’s Purpose (SOGP). SOGP will help you gain clarity about God’s truth, discover His purpose for your life, and grow to fulfil it. This short guide walks you through the journey ahead.

## Your SOGP Journey

Your SOGP journey has two phases: the Pre-SOGP phase and the SOGP phase itself.

### The Pre-SOGP phase

The Pre-SOGP phase begins the moment you enrol for SOGP, and your welcome message is your first lesson. It runs for the 14 days leading up to your cohort. Each day, as you count down to SOGP, you are expected to take part in:

1. Morning Prayer Watch (5:30 am West African Time)
2. Video Lesson of the day
3. Podcast review

### The SOGP phase

When your cohort for the School of God’s Purpose begins, your daily schedule will be:

1. Morning Prayer Watch (5:30 am West African Time)
2. Audio teaching of the day
3. Assessment of teaching

### Live Weekend Sessions

On the weekends you will also join two live sessions:

1. Q&A Session
2. Weekly teaching session

## Staying on Track

If you miss some days, don’t panic. It does not mean you have fallen behind or failed. To keep going, simply reopen that day on your dashboard and pick up from where you left off.

Also, you’re part of a community and the Telegram group is our home base for announcements, reminders, and links.

Morning Prayer Watch is live-streamed on YouTube so you can join from wherever you are.

If anything ever feels unclear, please reach out to our Community Managers. Their handles are published on the channel, and they would be glad to help.
