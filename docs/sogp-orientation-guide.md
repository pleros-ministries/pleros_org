# SOGP orientation guide

## How the programme works and how to use your dashboard

Welcome to the School of God’s Purpose (SOGP).

You have taken an important step towards finding answers, gaining clarity about God’s purpose for your life, and growing to fulfil it.

SOGP gives you clear teaching, practical guidance, prayer, and accountability within a flexible learning rhythm. This guide explains what happens before the programme begins, what to expect during the four course weeks, how to use your dashboard, and what you need to complete.

## Your SOGP journey at a glance

1. **Complete your enrolment and open your Welcome Pack.** This gives you the first steps for your cohort.
2. **Join the SOGP Telegram group.** Important orientation messages, reminders, links, and updates will be shared there.
3. **Complete Pre-SOGP.** The 30-day preparation period helps you build the rhythm you will need for the main programme.
4. **Complete the four SOGP levels.** Each level runs from Monday to Sunday and contains six teachings plus a Sunday review.
5. **Complete the learning and participation requirements.** Your dashboard records your assessments, Morning Prayer Watch attendance, and Sunday reviews.
6. **Receive your digital SOGP certificate when you become eligible.**

## Before the course: Pre-SOGP

Pre-SOGP begins 30 consecutive days before your cohort starts. It helps you build a steady rhythm of learning and prayer before the main programme begins. Every day has two requirements:

- a preparation lesson;
- 5:30 am Morning Prayer Watch, joined live or completed using the available replay.

Open the day on your Pre-SOGP calendar, complete both activities, and use the buttons on the page to record them. Watching or downloading a lesson does not mark it complete automatically.

Future lessons open on their assigned dates. If you miss a day, return to the red date on your calendar and complete it. Your Pre-SOGP lessons remain available after the main programme begins.

You can also turn on browser notifications. When enabled, your device receives one reminder at 5:20 am WAT, ten minutes before Morning Prayer Watch.

## The four-week SOGP rhythm

SOGP follows a fixed four-week rhythm that gives you clarity and accountability while leaving room for work, school, business, and family life. The programme has four sequential levels. Each level contains six required teachings and one required Sunday review.

### Monday to Saturday

Each day contains:

- **Teaching:** listen to or download the day’s teaching;
- **Morning Prayer Watch:** join live or use the available replay, then record your attendance;
- **Assessment:** complete the assessment attached to the teaching.

Your assessment, not audio playback, is the official completion record for a teaching. Where an assessment contains a quiz and a written response, complete both parts as instructed.

### Sunday

Sunday contains:

- Morning Prayer Watch;
- the required weekly review, completed live or using the recording provided.

No new teaching is released on Sunday.

## How to use your dashboard

### 1. Top navigation

The dark-blue bar shows whether you are in **PRE-SOGP** or **SOGP**. Select **Dashboard** to return to your main Pleros dashboard.

### 2. Calendar

The calendar is the quickest way to move between course days. It first shows the selected learning week. Select **Expand** to view the full calendar, then select a date to open its activities.

- **Green:** everything required for that date is complete.
- **Red:** the date has passed and at least one requirement is incomplete.
- **Grey:** the date is upcoming, or it is today and something is still incomplete.
- **Outlined date:** the day currently selected on your screen.

A red day does not mean you have failed the programme. Open it, see what remains, and complete the outstanding activity when you can.

### 3. Current level and track

At the top of the daily content, you will see your current level and position, such as **Level 2 · Track 3 of 6**. On Sundays, this area shows **Review day**.

### 4. Daily activity sections

Each activity appears in its own section:

- **Teaching** contains the audio and download option;
- **5:30 am Prayer Watch** links to Pleros Live and includes the attendance button;
- **Assessment** opens the day’s assessment and shows whether it is complete;
- **Required live review** appears on Sunday with the live or recording link.

Select an activity heading to open or close it. On mobile, the calendar appears above these activities. On desktop, the calendar and course outline appear beside them.

### 5. Course progress

The progress area shows three separate measures:

- teachings completed out of 24;
- Morning Prayer Watch attendance percentage;
- Sunday reviews completed out of four.

These measures show both your learning progress and your participation throughout the programme, so check all three regularly.

## How completion and unlocking work

SOGP is guided by dates and levels, but it also gives you room to catch up.

- A teaching does not open before its scheduled date.
- Level 1 opens when the cohort begins.
- Levels 2, 3, and 4 open only when their scheduled week has arrived **and** every assessment in the previous level is complete.
- Prayer Watch and Sunday reviews affect certificate eligibility, but they do not stop the next level from opening.
- If you complete a previous level late, any next-level teachings whose dates have already arrived become available immediately.
- You may continue incomplete work after the cohort’s four scheduled weeks.

If a track is locked, the dashboard will tell you which preceding level must be completed.

## What counts towards your certificate

To receive your digital SOGP certificate, you must complete:

- all 24 teaching assessments;
- at least 80% of Morning Prayer Watch across the 28 cohort dates;
- all four Sunday reviews, live or by recording.

The Pleros Podcast is available as a separate experience. Podcast activity does not count towards SOGP progress or certificate eligibility.

## Using the Telegram group

Keep the SOGP Telegram group pinned throughout the programme. It is the programme’s shared communication point for orientation, announcements, reminders, schedule updates, and important links.

- Read pinned messages before asking a question.
- Follow the instructions given by the programme team for each activity.
- Keep messages relevant to SOGP and be respectful to other learners.
- Do not post passwords, verification codes, personal records, or other sensitive information in the group.
- If questions are invited, send them clearly by text or voice note and include the teaching or day you are asking about.

Your dashboard remains the official place for course activities and completion records. A Telegram reaction or message does not mark an activity complete.

## If something does not look right

- Confirm that you are signed in with the email address used for your SOGP enrolment.
- Refresh the page and check your internet connection.
- Reopen the date and confirm that the activity now shows **Complete**.
- If a teaching is locked, check whether the scheduled date has arrived and whether the previous level’s assessments are complete.
- If you still need help, contact the programme team privately. Include your name, enrolment email address, the affected date, and a screenshot. Do not share sensitive details in the Telegram group.
