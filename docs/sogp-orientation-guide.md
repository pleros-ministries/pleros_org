# SOGP orientation guide

## How the programme works and how to use your dashboard

Welcome to the School of God’s Purpose (SOGP).

SOGP will help you gain clarity about God’s truth, discover His purpose for your life, and grow to fulfil it. This guide explains the course rhythm, how to use your dashboard, and what you need to complete.

[Open your Pleros Dashboard](https://pleros.org/dashboard)

## How SOGP works

### Pre-SOGP

Pre-SOGP begins 30 days before your cohort starts. Each day has two activities:

- a preparation lesson;
- 5:30 am Morning Prayer Watch, joined live or completed using the available replay.

After completing an activity, use the button on your dashboard to record it. Watching a lesson does not mark it complete automatically.

Future lessons open on their assigned dates. If you miss a day, return to it and complete the outstanding activities when you can. You can also turn on browser notifications to receive a reminder at 5:20 am WAT.

### The four-week programme

SOGP has four levels. Each level follows this weekly rhythm:

### Monday to Saturday

- Complete one teaching;
- Join Morning Prayer Watch;
- Complete the teaching assessment.

Your assessment—not audio playback—is the official completion record for a teaching.

### Sunday

- Join Morning Prayer Watch;
- Complete the required weekly review, live or using the recording.

The programme is scheduled across four weeks, but you may complete outstanding work after your cohort ends.

## How to use your dashboard

[Open your Pleros Dashboard](https://pleros.org/dashboard)

### Calendar

Select a date to open its activities. Select **Expand** to view the full calendar.

- **Green:** everything required for that date is complete.
- **Red:** the date has passed and something remains incomplete.
- **Grey:** the date is upcoming, or today is still incomplete.
- **Outlined date:** the date currently selected.

A red date does not mean you have failed. Open it and complete what remains.

### Daily activities

Your selected day may include:

- **Teaching:** the day’s lesson;
- **5:30 am Prayer Watch:** the Pleros Live link and attendance action;
- **Assessment:** the activity that completes the teaching;
- **Required review:** the Sunday live or recorded review.

On mobile, the calendar appears above the activities. On desktop, it appears beside them.

### Progress

Your dashboard tracks:

- teachings completed out of 24;
- Morning Prayer Watch attendance;
- Sunday reviews completed out of four.

A new level opens when its scheduled week arrives and the previous level’s assessments are complete.

## What counts towards your certificate

To receive your digital SOGP certificate, you must complete:

- all 24 teaching assessments;
- at least 80% of Morning Prayer Watch across the 28 cohort dates;
- all four Sunday reviews, live or by recording.

The Pleros Podcast is available as a separate experience. Podcast activity does not count towards SOGP progress or certificate eligibility.

## Using the Telegram group

Keep the SOGP Telegram group pinned throughout the programme. It is where you will receive orientation, announcements, reminders, schedule updates, and important links.

- Read pinned messages before asking a question.
- Keep messages relevant to SOGP and be respectful to other learners.
- Do not post passwords, verification codes, or sensitive personal information.
- When questions are invited, include the teaching or course day you are asking about.

Your dashboard remains the official place for course activities and completion records. A Telegram reaction or message does not mark an activity complete.

## If something does not look right

- Confirm that you are signed in with the email address used for your SOGP enrolment.
- Refresh the page and check your internet connection.
- Reopen the date and confirm that the activity now shows **Complete**.
- If you still need help, contact the programme team privately with the affected date and a screenshot.
