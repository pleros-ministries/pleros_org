PLEROS  ·  SOGP IMPLEMENTATION
Pleros SOGP Implementation Notes
Oct 7, 2026 • Implementation planning brief
Implementation direction
Extend the existing follow-up ownership and discipleship features. The immediate priority is to connect a new SOGP enrolment to an accountable, available person, make follow-up visible, and give leadership useful oversight of recorded activity.
The recording explicitly rejects automatic discipleship-group membership. Assignment establishes responsibility for contacting the participant. The owner then talks with the participant and shares the link for the intended group; the existing invitation and join flow determines membership.
This brief distinguishes requests from the recording, verified repository behavior, and recommended engineering design. The recommendations are a build plan, not approved organizational policy. Roster eligibility, routing precedence, oversight authority, and review cadence still need confirmation.

Recommended implementation design
The following data names and API responsibilities are proposals. Reuse equivalent existing structures where the final implementation audit finds them.
Assignment and follow-up
Keep pastor_assignments as the canonical enrolment-to-owner relationship. Add an explicit routing service used by enrolment, retries, and backfill. Resolve the agreed policy against approved staff and availability, persist the selected owner and policy reason, and leave a visible unassigned reason when nobody qualifies. Do not infer staff eligibility from referral attribution or group leadership.
Store assignee availability separately from permission to view records. Availability should pause new allocation without silently removing existing assignments. Confirm who maintains the roster and whether membership is opt-in or default-on. If round robin is selected, allocate atomically so concurrent enrolments do not repeatedly choose the same person or create duplicate owners.
Extend contact logging with a follow-up event history linked to the existing assignment and enrolment. Proposed fields are actor, contact time, channel, outcome, short necessary note, and next action or due date. Preserve existing contact count and last-contacted fields as consistent summaries. Distinguish not attempted, no response, responded, and declined; confirm the final labels and handling of declined follow-up with the product owner.
The staff UI should show the current owner, assignment reason, last contact, next action, and authorized participant details. An admin view should expose unassigned people and failed allocation attempts. Reassignment should be an explicit authorized action with an audit record; backfill must not overwrite current owners by default.
Reliable jobs and notifications
Persist work that must survive request completion. A transactional outbox or equivalent durable mechanism should connect assignment changes to owner notifications. Retry safely, record terminal failures, and expose operational status. Use the existing notification channels where suitable; keep the message minimal and link to the authorized record rather than copying sensitive pastoral content.
Add a reconciliation job for eligible enrolments without an owner and a controlled backfill operation for new region configuration. Both should be idempotent. A repeat enrolment hook, a retry, and a backfill racing together must still yield one owner and one notification per assignment event. Scheduling frequency and response-time targets remain product decisions.
Oversight and automatically assembled reports
Represent the confirmed supervisor-to-assignee relationships separately from learner discussion groups. A proposed oversight relationship records supervisor, subordinate, active dates, and who changed it. Validate cycles and ambiguous active parents unless the approved organization explicitly permits multiple supervisors. Reuse a current ministry or organizational hierarchy only if its meaning and access scope match this workflow.
Provide server-side report queries for a chosen period and the caller’s permitted reporting subtree. Proposed report content is assigned count, unassigned work, attempted contact, responses, outstanding next actions, and available participation context. Provide drill-down to permitted source records and show generation time, period, and missing-data indicators. Automatically assembling a report does not replace the human recording of activity.
Show the agreed reviewer, assignee, and next review date where one is recorded. Do not add recurring calendar invitations, external meeting integration, escalation deadlines, or a mandatory ministry-report field based on the unclear closing discussion. Confirm cadence and field definitions first. Generate reports on demand initially; add scheduled snapshots only if meeting preparation requires them.
Multiple discipleship groups
Remove the restriction that a leader can own only one group through a reviewed migration and query update. Preserve existing group identifiers, members, invitations, and history. Keep the current capacity, unique invitation code, learner membership constraint, and leader/member phone-consent behavior unless explicitly changed. Existing group-ID-based discussion, post, notification, invitation, and cron behavior is largely reusable.
Change singular dashboard and community group assumptions to a list of led groups. Add a group selector and Create another group flow with an editable freeform name; include a rename operation. Every group must have its own invitation link, and all reads and mutations must carry the intended group identifier. Audit assumptions that the leader’s first or only group is always the correct group. Ownership and membership checks must remain server-side. Each leader operation must validate the selected group before returning members, prompts, prayer requests, contacts, or private activity.
The invitation action should let the acting follow-up owner choose one of their own groups and share its link after contact. Do not expose private group contents to staff selecting a link. Persist a type or theme only if useful; a fixed taxonomy is not dictated by the recording. Assignment, reassignment, and creation of a default group must never insert group memberships or accept invitations. Joining or leaving a group must not silently change the accountable follow-up owner.
Thirty day podcast plans and progression
Reuse the existing content catalogue and participation records. Model a plan as an ordered 30-day schedule with one or two episodes per day, a default or tailored origin, an author, and a version. This is a proposed representation; check existing plan structures before adding tables.
Allow the authorized discipler to tailor choices to participant-stated interests without collecting unnecessary personal details. Preserve completion history when a plan changes, define how edits affect future days, and make the chosen schedule visible to the learner. Verify the catalogue can support the intended preset before promising 60 distinct episodes. Exact listening-duration thresholds, completion rules, and progression rules need explicit definitions.
Access and privacy
Apply authorization in queries, mutations, reports, exports, and notifications. A shared learner group does not authorize access to another person’s pastoral record. An oversight relationship must grant only the approved scope; do not assume every supervisor can read all private notes, prayer requests, or personal questions. Prefer aggregate oversight by default and separately authorize sensitive drill-down.
Preserve existing leader and disciple contact-consent rules when reusing contact details. Use minimal activity notes, record accountable changes, and exclude incidental personal information from the recording. The work does not authorize importing a live roster, contacting participants, granting roles, sharing private reports, or deploying changes.
Server actions and query boundaries
Follow the current server-action and query conventions rather than adding an unrelated API stack. Suggested boundaries are:
* assign or reassign an enrolment: authenticated staff actor, approved routing policy, transaction, uniqueness, and audit event;
* record a follow-up event: verified owner or explicitly authorized supervisor, validated outcome and next action, consistent contact summaries;
* list an oversight report: period plus server-derived permitted scope, with restricted drill-down;
* create, rename, select, and invite to a group: selected group ID with exact leader ownership; existing join confirmation remains the membership boundary;
* save or revise a podcast plan: learner identity, authorized editor, validated catalogue references, version check, and preserved completion history.
Derive the actor from the authenticated session; never trust a submitted staff ID or requested hierarchy scope. Test these boundaries directly, including calls made outside the UI.
Build sequence
1. Confirm the approved roster, routing precedence, and reporting authority. Add regression tests around the current enrolment side effects and ownership invariants before changing behavior.
2. Implement reliable ownership allocation, an unassigned queue, availability, idempotent reconciliation and backfill, and immediate owner notification. Extend the existing follow-up log and queue.
3. Migrate leader group ownership to support multiple groups and complete selector, creation, and per-group invitation flows. Test every assignment path for absence of membership side effects.
4. Add the agreed oversight relationships, scoped reports, and review visibility using actual recorded events and existing participation data. Validate the report with real leaders using authorized test data.
5. Add the 30-day podcast-plan flow after confirming content and completion semantics. Run migration, authorization, concurrency, and end-to-end tests before any deployment request.
Acceptance checklist
* An eligible enrolment receives exactly one approved owner or a visible unresolved status; missing location mapping never silently ends follow-up.
* Paused or ineligible staff receive no new allocation. Referral attribution cannot grant access. Concurrent assignment and retry paths preserve ownership uniqueness.
* A new assignment produces one timely owner notification linked to the scoped record. Retry and reconciliation do not duplicate notifications or overwrite an existing owner.
* The staff queue records contact time, channel, outcome, and next action, while keeping contact count and last-contacted summaries consistent. No response and declined follow-up differ from no attempt.
* Authorized leaders can inspect agreed reporting relationships and current period activity. Unauthorized peers and unrelated leaders cannot query individual records or infer private activity from report counts.
* Reports expose their period, generation time, and underlying authorized events. Missing records are labeled as unknown, not treated as verified inactivity or completed work.
* One authorized leader can create at least two named groups, switch between them, and retrieve distinct valid invite links. Existing membership, capacity, and invitations survive migration.
* Assignment, reassignment, backfill, and default-group creation add no learner membership. Accepting or leaving a group does not change the follow-up owner.
* Per-group reads and writes reject unauthorized group identifiers. A link for one group never joins the learner to another.
* A learner can choose a 30-day, one- or two-per-day plan. Default and tailored plans are distinguishable, and editing future content preserves completed participation.
* Existing enrolment, invitation acceptance, check-ins, prayer requests, and other unrelated group behavior pass regression checks.
Decisions required before implementation
* Who is on the eligible roster, who maintains availability, and how does organizational leadership map to existing pastor permissions? The recording affirms leader and sub-leader eligibility with an unclear exception; confirm the actual roster.
* Does current location routing take precedence over eligible-referrer preference and round robin? What happens when a mapped owner is unavailable, and when may an admin reassign existing people?
* What is the authoritative oversight tree, which supervisors may see identifiable records, and which private fields remain restricted?
* What is the review cadence, agreed report schema, follow-up outcome vocabulary, and next-action policy? No escalation service level is specified.
* Does multiple group ownership preserve the current capacity of 12 and one active discipler per learner? Recommended default: preserve both.
* Who curates podcast presets, how is completion measured, and what happens to future days when a plan changes? The recording does not establish a precise daily duration or automatic advancement rule.
