Track 1
The Gospel – The Word of Truth


Section A: MCQs


1. What best explains why humans are confronted with persistent questions about truth and existence?
A. Exposure to religious teachings before independent reasoning
B. A built-in burden or existential design that compels inquiry into truth
C. Psychological instability arising from deep questioning
D. Social influence shaping concern about meaning


2. What characterizes the nature of belief in Christ in relation to the word of truth?
A. It results from persuasion and social influence
B. It is imposed through doctrinal authority
C. It follows hearing and recognizing truth as truth
D. It develops through gradual moral discipline


3. Which statement accurately describes the nature of truth?
A. Truth depends on human awareness to exist
B. Truth is established through consensus
C. Truth exists independently of human knowledge
D. Truth is determined by usefulness


4. Which approach establishes truth in matters within the observable world?
A. Revelation alone
B. Observation and reason
C. Institutional teaching
D. Subjective conviction


5. What conclusion follows from a finite and contingent world?
A. The world sustains itself necessarily
B. The world is self-explanatory
C. The world implies a cause with greater power, intelligence, and character
D. The world exists without origin


Section B: SAQs


1. Distinguish between what can be known by observation and reason and what requires revelation.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


Observation and reason: what, how, when, where, who (2)
Revelation: why (1)
Intention located in a mind/heart (1)


2. State the two primary conclusions derived from observing and reasoning about the world.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


Existence of God / necessary cause (2)
Presence of contradiction/deficiency (2)


3. Explain why the contradiction in the world necessitates divine resolution.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


Contradiction (death, darkness, evil) (1)
Cannot originate from a perfect origin (1)
Only God can resolve it (1)
Resolution expressed as salvation/redemption (1)


Section C: Answer Key and Marking Guide

2. C
3. C
4. B
5. C

Section A: MCQs

A. Observation and reason applied to human experience

A. They describe sequential stages of divine action
B. They are distinct aspects of divine planning
C. They are interchangeable expressions of the same reality
D. They represent human interpretations of divine intent
        
3. What is identified as the direct expression of God’s purpose?
A. Redemption from sin and judgment
B. Moral transformation of humanity
C. Adoption as sons (sonship)
D. Establishment of divine authority over creation
        
4. What is the doctrinal meaning of the term “huiothesia”?
A. Legal acceptance into a divine covenant
B. Moral qualification for divine approval
C. Placement into the state of sonship
D. Gradual transformation into divine likeness


5. How is sonship expressed in its twofold reality?
A. As relational fellowship and moral obedience
B. As being a child of God and an heir of God
C. As possessing divine nature and exercising authority
D. As receiving salvation and fulfilling destiny


Section B: SAQs


1. Distinguish between general revelation and personal revelation in establishing God’s purpose.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        General revelation precedes personal revelation (1)
        •        General revelation established from Scripture/Word of God (1)
        •        Personal revelation is individual-specific (1)
        •        Personal revelation must align with general revelation (1)


2. Define sonship using its two core components and explain their implications.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Child (teknon): fellowship / relationship / reproduction after God’s kind (2)
        •        Heir (kleronomos): assigned lot / inheritance / responsibility (2)


3. Explain the distinction between natural assignment and spiritual assignment within heirship.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Natural assignment: earthly responsibilities / work / service context (1)
        •        Spiritual assignment: ministry / role in body of Christ (1)
        •        Both are inheritance (assigned lot from God) (1)
        •        Both are to be fulfilled as service to the Lord (1)

A. An automatic outcome of being made new
B. A result of disciplined moral effort
C. An instruction that follows the reality of being new

3. What is the necessary condition for walking in the Spirit?
A. Continuous avoidance of sin
B. Growth through the knowledge of the word
C. Emotional sensitivity to spiritual realities
D. Participation in communal worship


4. What distinguishes the “doer of the word” from the “hearer only”?
A. The extent of exposure to the word
B. The effort to practice moral instructions
C. The response of continuing versus misreckoning
D. The ability to recall scriptural statements


5. What is the correct sequence for spiritual development presented in the teaching?
A. Walk → Growth → Nature
B. Growth → Nature → Walk
C. Nature → Growth → Walk
D. Knowledge → Walk → Growth


Section B: SAQs


1. Explain the relationship between being a new creation and the instruction to walk in the Spirit.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        New creation is a reality at faith (not an instruction) (1)
        •        Receiving the Spirit / sealing with the Spirit (1)
        •        Walking is an instruction, not automatic (1)
        •        Walking depends on growth (1)


2. Define the process of growth as presented in the manuscript.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Knowledge of the word / implanted word (2)
        •        Reckoning (logizomai – taking into account identity) (1)
        •        Continuing (not forgetting / abiding) (1)
        •        Presenting (paristemi – standing beside identity) (1)
        •        Renewing of the mind (1)


3. Explain the role of the mind in walking in the new creation.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Mind determines walk (not nature) (1)
        •        Futility/ignorance vs knowledge (1)
        •        Renewing of the mind leads to transformation (1)
        •        Consciousness/awareness of identity (not forgetful) (1)

B. A fixed goal set before a person to pursue

A. A continuous experience of inner peace
B. A gradual improvement of moral behavior
C. A race with a goal set before, to be pursued and finished

A. Consistency in religious practices
B. Awareness of spiritual identity
C. Definite goal, submission to order, and sustained drive

1. Explain the doctrinal meaning of God’s purpose as revealed in Ephesians 1.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Purpose = will (what God wants) (1)
        •        Purpose = good pleasure (what God likes) (1)
        •        Revealed as mystery (known only by revelation) (1)
        •        Expressed as sonship (huiothesia) (1)


2. Define discipline (egkrateuomai / egkrateia) in relation to fulfilling purpose.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Self-control / temperance (1)
        •        Definite goal (purpose set before) (1)
        •        Submission to order required to fulfill goal (2)
        •        Drive (intentional pursuit / slave-driving oneself) (2)


3. State and explain the three commitments required to fulfill purpose.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Commitment to local church (order and participation) (2)
        •        Commitment to devotion (prayer, word, growth) (2)
        •        Commitment to assignments (natural and spiritual tasks) (2)
