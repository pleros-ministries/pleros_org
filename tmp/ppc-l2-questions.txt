Track 1
Introduction to Doctrinal Summaries


Section A: MCQs


1. What is identified as the immediate need after receiving salvation and God’s purpose?
A. Initiating spiritual activities to fulfill purpose
B. Growth through discipleship to develop capacity
C. Exercising authority in spiritual matters
D. Engaging in ministry assignments
        
2. What is the primary function of discipleship in relation to God’s purpose?
A. To secure salvation through continued instruction
B. To maintain spiritual discipline in daily life
C. To bring the believer to the point of being as the master
D. To establish fellowship within the church


3. What does the term “katartizo” convey in the context of discipleship?
A. Gradual improvement through repeated practice
B. Alignment of beliefs within a community
C. Being brought to a defined completion point
D. Continuous learning without a fixed end


4. How is the destination of discipleship described in Ephesians 4:13?
A. Unity in conduct and consistency in service
B. Knowledge of doctrine and participation in ministry
C.  Complete knowledge and complete growth in consciousness
D. Spiritual maturity expressed through obedience and discipline
        
5. What is the correct relationship between knowledge and stature in growth?
A. Stature develops independently of knowledge
B. Knowledge alone produces full spiritual growth
C. Knowledge is the prerequisite, and stature develops through consciousness based on that knowledge
D. Stature determines the level of knowledge attained


Section B: SAQs


1. Explain the relationship between salvation, purpose, and growth.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Salvation leads to receiving the Spirit / entry into purpose (1)
        •        Purpose creates desire to fulfill (1)
        •        Growth required to fulfill purpose (1)
        •        Growth comes through discipleship (1)


2. Define discipleship using the concept of katartizo.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Discipleship as training/perfecting (1)
        •        Katartizo = bringing to a point / completion point (1)
        •        Goal: becoming as the master (1)
        •        Involves different starting points but same destination (1)


3. Distinguish between knowledge and stature in spiritual growth.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Knowledge: unity of the faith / knowledge of the Son of God (2)
        •        Stature: perfect man / measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ (2)
        •        Knowledge is prerequisite (1)
        •        Stature develops through consciousness / reckoning (faith stand) based on knowledge (1)


Section C: Answer Key and Marking Guide

1. B
2. C
3. C
4. C
5. C


SAQs:
• Award marks strictly based on inclusion of required doctrinal terms.
• Distinctions must be explicit: salvation vs growth, knowledge vs stature, discipleship vs purpose fulfillment.




Track 2
Bibliology


Section A: MCQs


1. What is identified as the foundational starting point of doctrine for growth?
A. Discipleship through church leadership
B. Bibliology as the basis for all doctrine
C. Personal spiritual discipline
D. The practice of ministry assignments


2. Why must the source of doctrine be defended?
A. To validate personal belief systems
B. To establish credibility before others
C. Because doctrine and its source both claim to be truth
D. To ensure consistency in teaching methods


3. On what basis does the Bible claim authority as the document of truth?
A. Its historical preservation across generations
B. Its acceptance by early church communities
C. The provable truth of its message and validation by Jesus
D. Its detailed account of human history


4. What constitutes the authoritative basis for revelation?
A. The teachings of church leaders and tradition
B. The Scriptures together with Jesus’ chosen apostolic witnesses
C. The consensus of early Christian communities
D. The philosophical reasoning of theologians
        
5. What is the correct sequence in establishing the authority of Scripture?
A. Scripture proves truth → truth validates Jesus → gospel confirms God
B. Truth is established → fulfilled in Jesus → Jesus validates Scripture and apostles
C. Apostles declare Scripture → Scripture reveals truth → truth proves the gospel
D. Church tradition defines Scripture → Scripture teaches truth → truth confirms Jesus


Section B: SAQs


1. Explain why bibliology is the foundational part of doctrinal summaries.
Marking Scheme (4 marks):


        •        Bibliology establishes the source of doctrine (1)
        •        Doctrine required for growth to fulfill purpose (1)
        •        Need to defend source of doctrine (Scriptures and apostles) (1)
        •        Foundation for all other doctrinal knowledge (1)


2. Outline the basis upon which the Bible is considered the authoritative document of truth.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Message (gospel and doctrine) claims to be truth (1)
        •        Truth is highest authority (existentially/rationally) (1)
        •        Gospel proves truth (God, need for redemption, fulfillment in Christ) (2)
        •        Jesus validates Old Testament Scriptures (1)
        •        Jesus authorizes apostles as witnesses (New Testament) (1)


3. Identify and explain the key components involved in handling Scripture according to bibliology.
Marking Scheme (6 marks):


        •        Language (Hebrew, Greek, Aramaic) (1)
        •        Literature (narrative, exposition, poetry) (1)
        •        History/historicity (1)
        •        Interpretation (rightly dividing / orthotomeo) (1)
        •        Leads to theology (biblical, systematic) (2)


Section C: Answer Key and Marking Guide


MCQs:
1. B
2. C
3. C
4. B
5. B


SAQs:
• Award marks only where specific doctrinal terms are used.
• Emphasis on precision: truth, validation, Scriptures, apostles, interpretation.
• General or vague answers without these terms do not meet requirements.
